# Agent Evidence Studio brand

The identity is wordmark-first. Use the standalone Proofmark only where the full logo does not fit, such as a favicon, compact navigation, or app icon.

## Assets

- `public/brand/agent-evidence-logo.svg` — primary logo for light backgrounds.
- `public/brand/agent-evidence-logo-light.svg` — primary logo for dark backgrounds.
- `public/brand/proofmark.svg` — compact mark for light backgrounds.
- `public/brand/proofmark-light.svg` — compact mark for dark backgrounds.
- `public/favicon.svg` — adaptive browser favicon.
- `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/icon-192.png`, and `public/icon-512.png` — platform icons.
- `public/og.png` — social sharing image.

## Colors

- Ink: `#10231E`
- Ivory: `#FFF8ED`
- Coral: `#FF675B`

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the Proofmark width. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or recolor individual elements. The coral square represents one captured evidence record and must remain coral in the primary identity.
